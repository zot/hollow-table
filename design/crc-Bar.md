# Bar
**Requirements:** R9, R18, R20, R153, R154, R161, R286-288, R293, R294

The bar along the top: the wordmark, supplies, the people at the table, and the furniture at the
right end.

## Does
- look (R286): a walnut-dark strip 92 px high: the wordmark and its build stamp (selectable), the
  paper supplies, the chips in a 4 × 2 grid (+1 smaller), the people, "You are", then the register,
  the Ledger and Boot Hill at the right end.
- take (R287): a supply is made and lifted at the press, its middle under the pointer (Drag); loose
  text starts "Write here"; the image supply opens a file picker, the image landing in the middle of
  the view, several 24 px apart.
- over (R288): while anything is carried over the bar it turns red (`toss`); if it would be buried,
  "→ <current graveyard>" shows by the gravestone (`bury`). Plain chips, +1s and the reference card
  vanish; an Influence card goes home.
- supplies: character, sticky, loose text, image (a file picker), the "How to play the table"
  card, and chips 2, 4, 6 and +1 in each sign; each starts a Drag.
- people (R293): a name tag per person in the hand font beside a dot, green and glowing when live;
  the GM's reddish, your own outlined; the GM first, then you, then alphabetical; dimmed after 25 s
  unheard, gone after 60 s; the GM's renames on click; a page that is nobody
  gets its "not yours" chip; the GM's strip marks such pages in red, and clicking one hands the name
  over.
- table: Open the table, then Copy invite (shift-click or long press closes it); "Copied" for about
  a second, or the link shown when the clipboard refuses (R294).
- "You are" (a real GM only): a tag that cycles through GM, the players present and the defaults;
  a player's page has none.
- build stamp under the wordmark.
