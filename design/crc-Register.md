# Register
**Requirements:** R99, R115, R116, R117

The cash register: the Round, and End.

## Does
- round: shown as a pop-up tab in the glass; ▲ ▼ change it (meta.round).
- end(): the first press raises the orange "End conflict?" tab for 3 s; a second press within them
  ends the Conflict, in one Game.change after a checkpoint: Round to 0, Biases and Afflictions
  recharged (Tap.recharge), used piles emptied (plain cards home, tapped cards gone), used marks
  cleared, CRIT! Influences cleared wherever they are.
