# Boot Hill

Where put-away work goes, so nothing that holds work is lost by accident.

- **Boot Hill** is a gravestone at the bar's right end. Its badge counts the buried; clicking it
  opens the list.
- **How it looks and reads** (Bill, 11-12, as the mockup): a grey stone with "Boot Hill" carved in
  Sancreek; the red badge counts everything buried in every graveyard, and the stone fades when
  nothing is. The list is oldest first, and an empty graveyard says "Nothing is buried here." A
  burial is named after its pool (or "Nameless"); a sticky "Note: " and its first line, loose
  writing "Writing: " and its first line (28 characters at most); a receipt by its title; a torn
  roll "Tape: N rolls, last" and the newest receipt's title.
- **An Info card the GM empties is buried** (see `ownership.md`), named "Devreux: Info card" after
  its NPC. Dragged out of the list it goes back on its NPC where it was, slid out; while its NPC isn't
  on the table it stays buried. Players see it as **"GM card"** (Bill, 11-18: "let the players see 'GM
  card' rather than 'Devreux: Info card'"), with nothing of what it says or whose it was; only the GM can bring it back or delete it.
- **An Opinion Record the GM empties is buried the same way** (see `connections.md`), named "Devreux:
  Opinion of Sam" after its NPC (or group) and character; dragged out it comes back under its NPC's
  card, and players see "GM card".
- **The list closes on a press anywhere else**, as the Ledger does (Bill, 11-12).
- **Putting away** a pool or its character card buries both, with everything on them. Stickies,
  loose writing, images and torn rolls are buried too.
- **A torn tape is buried on its own page** (Bill, 2026-11-23): dropped on the bar, a torn roll goes
  into the graveyard being browsed, on that page alone. It is listed there after the graveyard's
  other burials and counted in the badge, and no other page's Boot Hill shows it; it is dug up and
  deleted forever only there. If no graveyard has its graveyard's name any more, it shows in the first.
  Deleting everything in a graveyard deletes this page's buried tapes there too.
- **Drag a name out** of the list to bring it back under the pointer (a character by its pool, the
  card following). It comes up once it's dragged a few pixels, so a long press on it can delete it
  forever like a shift-click (Bill, 11-13), and the list closes. Dropped back on the bar, it's
  buried again.

## Graveyards

- Boot Hill holds **named graveyards**. The panel's header has ‹, the editable name, and ›, fixed in
  place while the list below scrolls.
- Burials go into the **current** graveyard; holding something over the bar shows "→ name". Moving
  things between graveyards is: drag out, flip, put away.
- › on the last graveyard makes a new one only if this one has something or a name of its own. A
  new graveyard is numbered **one past the highest "Graveyard N" in use**, so › ‹ doesn't count up.
- A graveyard that is **empty and nameless** (a never-changed name counts as nameless) disappears when
  its last item is dragged out, its name is erased, or you leave it. There is always at least one; the
  only one is kept even when empty and nameless.

## Deleting forever

- Shift-click an item, or a graveyard's name, to delete it (or everything in that graveyard)
  **forever**. It takes **two** shift-clicks: the first arms it red and says so, reverting after
  3 s, because it is the one thing that can't be undone.
