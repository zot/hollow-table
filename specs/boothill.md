# Boot Hill

Where put-away work goes, so nothing that holds work is lost by accident.

- **Boot Hill** is a gravestone at the bar's right end. Its badge counts the buried; clicking it
  opens the list.
- **Putting away** a pool or its character card buries both, with everything on them. Stickies,
  loose writing, images and torn rolls are buried too.
- **Drag a name out** of the list to bring it back under the pointer (a character by its pool, the
  card following). Dropped back on the bar, it's buried again.

## Graveyards

- Boot Hill holds **named graveyards**. The panel's header has ‹, the editable name, and ›, fixed in
  place while the list below scrolls.
- Burials go into the **current** graveyard; holding something over the bar shows "→ name". Moving
  things between graveyards is: drag out, flip, put away.
- › on the last graveyard makes a new one only if this one has something or a name of its own. A
  new graveyard is numbered **one past the highest "Graveyard N" in use**, so › ‹ doesn't count up.
- A graveyard that is **empty and nameless** (a never-changed name counts as nameless) disappears when
  its last item is dragged out, its name is erased, or you leave it. There is always at least one; the
  only one is kept even when empty and nameless.

## Deleting forever

- Shift-click an item, or a graveyard's name, to delete it (or everything in that graveyard)
  **forever**. It takes **two** shift-clicks: the first arms it red and says so, reverting after
  3 s, because it is the one thing that can't be undone.
