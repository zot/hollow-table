# The desk

Where work is kept without burying it: filed in the desk's drawers, found again, and brought back.
The desk itself and its pigeonholes are in `table.md`, *The bar*. From `carves/organizing.md`, Item 3.

## Filing

- **Dropped on the desk, a piece is filed in its drawer** (Bill, 2026-11-23: "Agreed"): anywhere else
  on the bar it is buried on Boot Hill, as before. Boot Hill is for throwing away, the desk for keeping.
- **The "→" hint names the drawer** while a piece is over the desk ("→ NPCs"), where over the rest of
  the bar it names the graveyard.
- **The drawers are fixed by kind** (Bill, 2026-11-23: "fixed by kind"). The GM's desk has three:
  **Characters** (a character a player owns, or an open one), **NPCs** (the GM's own characters), and
  **Papers** (stickies, loose text, images and dockets, a docket with its papers). A player's desk has **Papers** alone (Bill,
  2026-11-24: "Paper only"): a character is the GM's game, so a character a player drops on their desk
  is buried, as anywhere else on the bar.
- **What isn't filed** does on the desk what it does on the bar: plain chips vanish, an Influence card
  goes home, the reference card vanishes, the Connection Table is put away, a torn tape is buried on
  its own page.
- **A filed character takes its things with it** (Bill, 2026-11-24: "All go with it"), as a burial
  does: its card, the Influence cards of its drawer and pool, and the paper pinned to it. Its Info
  cards and what of it has been revealed go with its card. Its Opinion Records stay with its row of the
  Connection Table, which stays too, and show under its card again when it comes back.

## Whose drawers

- **The GM's drawers are the GM's.** They are kept on the GM's page with the game and never sent:
  every other page loses what the GM files, as it would a burial, and holds nothing of it while it is
  in the desk, not even its name.
- **A player's drawer is that player's alone** (Bill, 2026-11-23: "Player only"), kept on their page
  as a "just me" note is: never sent, kept through reloads there, and not in the game the GM saves, so
  clearing that browser loses it.

## Opening and bringing back

- **The open desk** (a click, `table.md`) shows its drawers below the pigeonholes: drawer fronts with
  brass pulls, each named on a small label. Clicking one pulls it out, listing what is filed there,
  oldest first, each by name as Boot Hill names a burial (a character by its name, "Note: " and the
  first line, "Writing: " and the first line, "Docket: " and its name, "Image"); an empty drawer says "Nothing filed here."
  Clicking it again, or another, closes it.
- **Drag a name out** of a drawer to bring it back under the pointer, with everything filed with it.
  It comes up once dragged a few pixels, as from Boot Hill. A PC comes back to its player (Bill,
  2026-11-24: "Back to its player"), owned as when it was filed.
- **Dropped back on the desk** it is filed again; on the rest of the bar, buried.

## Finding

From `carves/organizing.md`, Item 5 (Bill, 2026-11-24: "words too for a typed-in search but we should
also have a way to present a list of names to the GM", and "Empty search shows it").

- **The open desk has a search line** at the top of its drawers, written in the hand font. Typing in
  it lists what is filed that matches, as you type: every word typed, in any case, found in its name
  or in the words on what was filed (a note's or loose writing's words, a character card's front, back
  notes and Info cards, a docket's name and its papers' words). **Names first**: what matches by its
  name comes before what matches only by its words, each alphabetical. Nothing matching says "Nothing
  found."
- **With nothing typed, it is the list of names**: every name filed, in every drawer, alphabetical.
  Clearing what was typed brings the whole list back.
- **Each result says its drawer** ("NPCs") and drags out as from its drawer.
- **A page searches only its own desk**, so a player finds only what they filed.
